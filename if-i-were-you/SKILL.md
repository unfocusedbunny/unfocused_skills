# If I were you

Too many open threads, no idea where to start. This skill answers one question: if Claude were the user today, what would it actually do? Not a plan for the year. Today.

## Step 1: Know who you're talking to

Picks are only as good as what you know about the user. First check what you already have: memory, project files, past chats you can search, anything they shared in this chat.

You need two things:
- **who they are**: what they do, what they're juggling,
- **what they're working towards right now**: current goals, deadlines, what's stuck.

**If you don't have both, ask before picking.** One short message: they can upload or paste something that explains it (a CV, a goals note, a project list), or answer these:
1. What do you do, in a sentence or two?
2. What are you working towards right now? (1-3 things)
3. Any deadlines coming up?
4. How much time and energy do you have today?

Then stop and wait. Don't pick from guesses.

When they answer, offer once to keep it ("Want me to remember this, so next time I can skip the questions?"). If they say yes, save it in memory if you have it; otherwise suggest they paste it into their preferences or project instructions.

**If you already know enough,** skip the questions and go to step 2. Still ask about today's time and energy only if it would change the picks.

## Step 2: Pick 1-3 things for today

- **Real deadlines first.** If something is due today or tomorrow, a pick helps with that.
- **At least one pick takes 20 minutes or less**, so there's always a way to start.
- **Concrete and doable today.** "Send Anna the draft", not "work on networking".
- **Moves a goal forward.** Every pick ties to something they said they're working towards.
- **Fewer if they're overloaded.** If they have little time or energy, one pick is plenty.
- Don't revive things they've parked or dropped, and don't add new projects.

## Step 3: Say it short

For each pick:

**The pick** (rough time)
One line on why, tied to their goal.

Then one closing line saying what the picks are based on ("Based on: job hunt, portfolio, German. Tell me if that's changed.").

If you're unsure where something stands (maybe it's already done), say so in the pick: "guessing here, tell me if it's done."

## Rules

- Never invent facts about the user, their deadlines or other people. If you don't know, ask or say you're guessing.
- These are your suggestions, not their tasks. They decide.
- Informal, candid, short enough to read on a phone. No motivational speeches.
