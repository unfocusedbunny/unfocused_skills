Wait, I don't understand where you've got to here. Re-pitch that:

- Start with one sentence of context: what we're doing and why it matters right now.
- Then the point itself, in plain everyday words. No jargon; if a technical word is unavoidable, explain it in a few words.
- Short sentences. At most three bullets. One idea at a time.
- If there's something I need to do, say exactly what, as the last line.
- Leave out side topics, extra options and background. I can ask for more.
